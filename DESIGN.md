# Design system

How "Tivvy" looks and reads. Follow this when changing the app, and update it when Beth approves a new rule. Sizes are in CSS pixels (`styles.css`); colors are the tokens at the top of `styles.css`.

## Text sizes

The app uses one small scale. Each size has one job; avoid in-between sizes like 13.5 or 14.5.

| Size | Job | Where it's used |
|---|---|---|
| 22 | App title | "Tivvy", with the orange retro TV logo (28 wide) just before it; the welcome card title ("Welcome to Tivvy") |
| 20 | Screen titles | "Profile"; show title on a detail screen |
| 18 | Headings | Home row titles ("Trending now"), Settings section headings, detail-screen headings ("Why this pick") |
| 17 | Score numbers | Big numbers in the detail-screen score boxes ("9.0", "90%") |
| 16 | Form inputs, tip headings | Search box and "TV & movies" picker (16 stops iPhone from zooming in); the bold heading in the genre tip |
| 15 | Buttons and tappable labels | Add to list, Mark watched, Like, Not for me, backup buttons, "Pick your services", "How your picks work" item names, name in a detail screen's top bar, show names in search results |
| 14 | Body text and subtitles | Home row subtitles, Settings descriptions, "How your picks work" text, genre and service names, show description, "Why this pick" text, detail-screen year/length/genres line, welcome card text, filter buttons |
| 13 | Title details in lists | Show names under posters (posters are narrow, so 13 keeps long names readable); the year/type/rating and service lines in search results |
| 12 | Fine print | Year and rating under posters, score labels, service badges on the detail screen, "Liked", "In your list" and "Watched" pills in search, "Data updated" note, table headers, the "No longer available" note on posters, the grey source line under each signal in "Why this pick" |
| 12.5, capitals | Group labels inside a Profile section | "DATA FROM THE INTERNET" and "YOUR CHOICES, SAVED ON THIS PHONE" in "When data updates": grey, semi-bold, all capitals, slightly spaced out, like iPhone Settings, with 24 above and 8 below. The only all-capitals text in the app; written in sentence case and shown in capitals |
| 10.5 | Badges on posters | Service badge on a poster ("Netflix") |

Default text (anything not listed) is 15 with 1.4 line spacing. Paragraphs use 1.5 line spacing.

## Colors

| Token | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | #0f1115 | Page background |
| `--surface` | #181b22 | Cards, sheets (Settings, detail screen), welcome card |
| `--surface-2` | #222631 | Buttons, score boxes, picker tiles |
| `--text` | #f2f3f5 | Main text |
| `--muted` | #9aa1ad | Subtitles, descriptions, fine print |
| `--line` | #2b303b | Borders and dividers |
| `--accent` | #ffb547 (orange) | Main call to action only ("Pick your services"), checked boxes, the "Refresh" and "Undo" buttons on the bottom banners, the underline under the active tab on the Your library page (the one place it marks a selection), and one-time tips (dark text on the app color) |
| `--up` / `--down` | green / red | "Like" and "Not for me" when selected |

**Orange is not a "selected" or "focused" color.** The search box shows a thin grey (`--muted`) outline while you type. Nothing else ever shows a focus outline or tap flash: dropdowns, buttons, pills, posters and tabs just respond. The list and watched buttons stay white with a plain outline when on; the icon and wording show the state.

## Buttons

- Rounded corners: 10 for buttons, 999 (pill) for filter buttons, 50% for round icon buttons.
- Secondary buttons: `--surface-2` fill, 1px `--line` outline, white text, weight 600, size 15.
- Main call to action: orange fill, dark text, weight 700. Sized to its label (not full width) on the welcome card.
- **Trailers:** when a title has a trailer, its screen shows a full-width "Watch trailer" row right under the poster and title (below whichever is taller), 16 above the scores: a pill (999 corners, 1px `--line` outline, `--surface-2` fill) with orange 14 semi-bold text and a small drawn play triangle, centered. It opens the official trailer on YouTube (the YouTube app if installed). Titles without a trailer show nothing there. Also tried: a small see-through play button on the poster (no label, like Netflix), which is easy to switch back to; a yellow button (too loud), text only (too faint), and pills inside the title column (crowded it, and moved around with long titles). Trailers come from TMDB (official YouTube trailers first, then any trailer, then a teaser) and are collected by the daily full refresh.
- **Score tiles that open a website:** on a title's screen, the IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic tiles are tappable. They show a small drawn arrow (↗, 14 wide, the usual "opens a website" sign) in a light grey (#c6cad1, halfway between `--muted` and `--text`) in their bottom-right corner, where the eye finishes reading the tile, and a soft light glow rising from that corner (white at 10% fading to clear 70% of the way across the tile) over the usual tile color. No orange, outline or tap flash. Also tried: the same in orange (an orange arrow alone, and with an orange glow at 20% or softened to 10% with a 70% arrow; the orange added up right under "Watch trailer"), an arrow pointing right (→; reads as "next screen in the app"), the arrow in the top-right corner, a bigger arrow (17 wide), a grey arrow without the glow (easy to miss), a `--muted` grey arrow with an 8% glow (a little faint), the arrow after the site name, an outline, a chevron, a brighter site name, a lighter background, a drop shadow, an even yellow tint and a yellow edge. Each opens that site's page for the title. IMDb pages come from the IMDb id we already have; exact Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic pages come from Wikidata, collected by the daily full refresh. Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic are tappable only when their exact page is known, so the arrow always means "straight to the page". Linking to their search pages was tried and dropped: Rotten Tomatoes lists movies first and buries shows. The Trakt, TMDB, Netflix Top 10 and "New on streaming" tiles aren't tappable (their pages add little). Links open in iPhone's built-in browser page (a home-screen app can't choose otherwise); ✕ comes straight back, and its compass button opens Safari.
- **No "Watch on" button on the detail screen.** It was tried and removed: services don't share title links, so the buttons were unreliable, and people go to their service's app (often on the TV) anyway. The service badges show where to watch.
- Button labels stay on one line on every iPhone, including the smallest (iPhone SE). Trim side padding before shrinking text.

## Spacing

- Screen side margin: 16.
- Space between home screen rows: 28 above each row title; 14 between a row's subtitle and its posters.
- Gap between side-by-side buttons: 10; between tiles in a grid: 6 to 8.
- Paragraphs in "How your picks work" (Settings and the Top Picks page): 14 between paragraphs; 16 above and below charts.
- Welcome screen: behind the card, a tilted (-8°), darkened (38%) wall of today's trending posters, five across, filling the whole screen below the header, with a dark gradient rising from the bottom (solid page color at the very bottom, clear by about halfway up) and a softer one under the header (clear by 30% down). The card's middle sits 45% of the way down the screen (a little above center, which looks centered to the eye), over the wall, slightly see-through (86% of the card color, lightly blurred), 34 in from each side (on small phones it widens to 16 in, so the text keeps two lines) with 50 top, 58 bottom and 26 side padding; 44 top and bottom, 24 sides, 28 above its button, everything centered. Title 22 bold, text 15, button 16. At the top, the orange retro TV logo (the header logo's drawing, 48 wide), 22 above the title. Title "Welcome to Tivvy" (no exclamation mark), 22 bold. Text: "Discover what to watch. The best shows and movies, picked just for you." (positive and action-first; no openers like "Never" or "No more") on two balanced lines (no single word left alone). Button "Get started". It opens Profile with one extra line at the top (15, main text color, 18 below the header), shown only when opened from the welcome card: "Pick your services, then tap 👍 on a few genres you love." Streaming services and Genres sit right below it, so picking both is one step. The Genres description doesn't call genres optional. The welcome screen has no header at all (the poster wall runs to the top of the screen); the header, with search and Settings (a person icon), appears once services are picked.

## Writing

- Plain, short and friendly. Sentence case ("Add to list", not "Add To List").
- **"Top Picks" is a name,** so both words are capitalized wherever it's mentioned ("tune your Top Picks", "How Top Picks work"). The one exception is the home row title itself, "Top picks for you", which stays in sentence case like every row title (and so does the "Top picks for you" item in "How your picks work", which names that row).
- **Row subtitles:** one short line that says what the row is. Full explanations live in Settings under "How your picks work". Subtitles may wrap to two lines on small phones; never cut them off with "…".
- **No orphans:** a heading or subtitle that wraps never leaves one word alone on its last line; the last two words always stay together ("Because you liked The Haunting of / Hill House"). Applies to home row titles and subtitles, "See all" page titles and subtitles, and the title on a title's screen.
- **Periods:** leave the period off a subtitle or description that is a single sentence. Keep periods when there are two or more sentences.
- The Top Picks subtitle, "Highly rated and new, tuned to your likes", stays the same before and after the user rates titles. "Likes" hints at what makes the row personal (👍 on titles and genres) without explaining it; the tips and "How it works" do that.
- Use "list" in buttons ("＋ Add to list", then a bookmark icon + "Added to list") "Watchlist" for the saved titles, and "Your library" for the home row and page that hold the watchlist, liked and watched titles.
- Use "＋" for an action that adds ("＋ Add to list"). Show a state that's on with its icon: the bookmark for the list ("Added to list", "In your list"), "✓" for watched ("✓ Watched").

## Icons

- **App icon:** "Glowing TV": the retro TV (rabbit-ear antennas, rounded screen, play arrow) in thick lines that fade from gold (#ffd27a) at the antennas to deep orange (#f2801f) at the bottom, on a near-black background that darkens gently toward the bottom (#171a21 to #0b0c10; the earlier charcoal fade, #262b36 to #0f1115, looked washed out at the top on a dark wallpaper), sitting a little above center. The TV, antennas included, spans about 60% of the icon's width (85% of its original size), leaving some space around it without looking small. The original size filled about 70% and looked crowded; 78% of it looked too small on the home screen, and 70% was also tried. It matches the dark app with its orange logo. Tried and passed over: flat orange, an orange "Sunset" fade, cream and white backgrounds. The header logo (28 wide) and the welcome-card logo (48 wide) are the same glowing TV, with the same gold-to-orange fade, on the dark app; only the logo has the fade (buttons and other orange accents stay flat); the launch screen shows just the TV, 80 wide, centered on the white launch screen, fading from orange (#ffb547) to a deeper orange (#e8661a), with no circle or dark background (the icon's lighter gold washes out on white).

- **Link preview:** `icons/preview.png` (1200×630): the glowing TV logo (gold to orange, like the app icon) and "Tivvy" in large white type on the page color, with a soft orange glow. No tagline in the image, since the description shows under it. Preview title "Tivvy: Discover what to watch" (iMessage shows only the image, the title and the web address, not the description; the page title and home-screen name stay "Tivvy"), description "Discover what to watch. The best shows and movies, picked just for you." The preview tags use full web addresses.

- Icons are simple drawn shapes in the style of the search and settings buttons, not emoji (emoji look different on each phone).
- **Header buttons:** search (21 wide) and Profile (an outline person icon, 20 wide), each in a 40 round button. The person icon opens "Profile" (the settings panel), with plain section headings in this order: "Streaming services", "Genres", "Back up your data", "How your picks work", "When data updates", "Share Tivvy". Things you change come first, then keeping your data safe, then explanations, then sharing at the bottom (like iPhone Settings).
- **Poster status circles:** small white icons in dark circles in the poster's top-right corner, always in this order: **liked** (drawn thumbs-up), **in your list** (bookmark), **watched** (✓). Watched posters are never dimmed; the ✓ is the only marker (dimming means "no longer available", below). Each icon is left out where every poster would have it: no bookmark in "Your library" or the Watchlist tab, no thumb in the Liked tab, no ✓ in the Watched tab.
- **Not for me** is never marked on posters (those titles are hidden from rows).
- **Search results** show the same states as small grey pills under the service line: "Liked" (thumb), "In your list" (bookmark), "Watched" (✓).

## Behavior rules tied to the design

How titles are picked and ordered (scores, rows, likes, rotation) is documented in full in [ALGORITHM.md](ALGORITHM.md); the rules below cover how that looks and behaves on screen.

- Marking a title watched doesn't change any other button. A watched title stays on the list and keeps showing in "Your library" (with the ✓ circle, not dimmed), but leaves every other row except the Netflix Top 10.
- A watched TV series counts as unwatched again once a season premieres after the date it was marked watched, so the new season shows in the rows. Marking it watched again hides it until the next season.
- **"Because you liked…" row:** right below Your library, once you've liked or saved a title. Subtitle "Similar shows and movies you might enjoy" (no "services": every row is on your services). It shows titles TMDB recommends or lists as similar to one title (either way round), best match first, with liked, watched and Not for me titles left out; disliked genres and rotation apply. Which title: liked titles first (newest first), titled "Because you liked [title]"; then watchlist titles that aren't liked, disliked or watched, titled "More like [title]" (never "liked" for a title you've only saved). Watched titles only count when liked. Only titles with at least 6 similar titles qualify; it changes the first time the app is opened on a new day. During a visit it changes only when its title stops being a favorite (removed from both likes and the watchlist): then it moves on right away (to the next title, "Based on your favorites", or no row). Its wording also updates right away if the title is unliked but still saved ("More like …"), or the other way. A new like doesn't make it jump; it joins the line, and newest likes come first. Like Your library, the page shifts so nothing on screen moves. With none, the row is "Based on your favorites" (it covers likes and the watchlist; not "Based on your likes", which left out the list, or "More like what you liked": "like" twice), pooling similar titles from all of them (needs 4). Nothing liked or saved, no row. Its "See all" sorts by "Best match for you". Explained in "How your picks work".
- **Keeping rows fresh (rotation):** a home-row poster counts as seen once per day when it's mostly (60%) on screen for about a second with nothing open on top. From the 4th day a title is seen (any days, not necessarily in a row) without being opened, liked, saved or marked watched, it moves 4 places down its row per day (behind the title already in that spot); it never disappears, it just moves along (and out of the first 20 after about 9 days seen). It started on the 3rd day at first; 4 gives people a few days to consider a title before it moves. It only changes when rows are drawn, never during a visit (today's view counts from tomorrow). After 2 weeks unseen it's back in its usual spot with a fresh start. Opening a title clears its count and keeps it in place for 2 weeks. Applies to Top Picks, Trending, Top rated, Critics' favorites and New (and their "See all" pages), before disliked genres sink; not to Your library or the Netflix Top 10. Settings are in config.json (`rotation`: start_day 4, places_per_day 4, reset_days 14). Explained in "How your picks work" under "Keeping rows fresh".
- **Disliked genres sink in every row:** a genre 👎 in Profile moves its titles behind all the others in every row (Top Picks, Trending, Top rated, Critics' favorites, New, and their "See all" pages), keeping each row's own order otherwise. A liked genre wins: a title that's in both a disliked and a liked genre stays put. Only Profile genres count (not 👎 on single titles, which stay a gentle Top Picks signal). Your library and the Netflix Top 10 aren't affected. Liked genres only boost Top Picks.
- **Not for me is about the title, not its genres.** It hides the title and lowers the titles TMDB lists as similar to it in Top Picks, but never its genres (one disliked drama shouldn't push down every drama; the genre 👎 in Profile is for that). Likes do raise their genres in Top Picks, but common genres count less (each counts (1 − its share of titles)³: about 25% for Drama, 45% for Comedy, 60% for Crime, nearly fully for rarer ones), so one liked drama adds a few dramas to Top Picks instead of filling it (tried: full strength, 40% and 15% for Drama; 15% made drama Likes barely matter).
- "Not for me" (👎) titles are hidden from every row except "Your library" and the Netflix Top 10. Search still finds them so the rating can be undone.
- **Your library page** (opened from the "See all ›" tile, or the pills before anything is saved): full screen like search, with a round back arrow (‹) to the left of the title "Your library". Three tabs as plain words with an underline ("Watchlist", "Liked" and "Watched", no counts): 14 padding on each side, the active tab white with a 2-high orange underline spanning its padding, a faint full-width line under both; 20 below the line and 20 below the filter pills, then a scrolling bar with the sort and TV & movies pickers and service chips, then posters three across. A tab with nothing in it hides the sort and filters and shows, centered about 110 below the tab line: the tab's icon (28, white) in a 64 `--surface-2` circle, a bold 18 heading 24 below it, then a grey 15 message 10 below that, naming the button to tap in bold white (kept on one line). Watchlist: "Nothing saved yet" / "Tap **＋ Add to list** on any title to save it here." Liked: "Nothing liked yet" / "Tap **Like** on any title to see it here." Watched: "Nothing watched yet" / "Tap **Mark watched** on any title to see it here." Watched titles appear only in the Watched tab (not "Watchlist"), where posters aren't marked watched. The Liked tab shows every title you've liked, listed or watched or not; it sorts by Recently liked (default; likes from before dates were saved come last), Best match, IMDb rating, Newest release or A–Z. Closing a title opened from this page updates it right away, keeping the scroll position (a poster that leaves fades out in 0.15 seconds, then the posters after it glide into place in 0.2 seconds, like a home row closing its gap): a title removed from the list leaves "Watchlist"; one marked watched moves to Watched; one un-marked in Watched goes back to "Watchlist" if it's on the list, or simply leaves; one un-liked leaves the Liked tab.
- **Home rows** show up to 20 titles (`row_length` in config.json). When a row has more, a "See all ›" tile ends it (poster-sized, card color with a thin outline, 15 semi-bold, same as in "Your library"). It opens that row in full on its own page: full screen like Your library, with a round back arrow (‹) and the row title, the row's grey 14 subtitle 14 below the header line, then 16 below that the scrolling bar with a sort picker (the row's own order first: "Best match for you", "Most popular", "Audience rating", "Critic rating", "Recently added"; then IMDb rating, Newest release, A–Z), the TV & movies picker and service chips (starting from the home screen's), then posters three across: 100 to start, with the next 100 added quietly when you're within two screens of the end, until the list runs out; a long list (over 100) ends with a grey 14 "That's everything", 28 below the grid. "See all" (and the Your library page) always opens fresh at its top, closing any title screen left open behind it, so going back always returns to the home screen. Home rows don't load more as you swipe: the best titles come first, and "See all" is the place to dig deeper.
- **Grid pages load a screen at a time, not in a cascade.** Opening a grid page (Your library, a row's "See all") or changing its tab, sort or filter keeps the old grid, dimmed to 40%, with a small grey spinner in the tile area, 40% of the way from the top of the grid to the bottom of the screen (a little above center, which looks centered to the eye). The spinner only appears if the wait passes 0.12 seconds, so changes are instant when the posters are already on the phone; once it appears it stays at least 0.3 seconds so it never flickers. This applies to opening a page and to changing a tab, sort or filter, until the posters for the first screen (12) have loaded and are ready to draw, then shows the new grid all at once with those posters already in place, with one soft 0.15-second fade. It never waits more than 3 seconds. Posters further down fade in (0.2 seconds) as you scroll to them.
- **"How it works ›" on Top Picks only:** grey 14 text link on the right edge of the "Top picks for you" title line. It opens "How Top Picks work" full screen (like search) with a ✕, showing the same explanation and chart as Settings. Other rows rely on their subtitles.
- **Title screen length:** the grey line under a title's name says how long it is. Movies: "1974 · Movie · 3h 22m · Drama, Crime" (under an hour: "45m"). Shows: "2022–2025 · 2 seasons · Drama, Mystery", where the season count replaces "TV series" (it already says it's a show, and the years alone can mislead, since shows often take long breaks). Only seasons that have aired count; announced ones are left out of both the count and the years. Without the data it falls back to "Movie" or "TV series".
- **Title screen top:** the poster (110 wide) always keeps its 2:3 shape and sits at the top. A long title wraps onto as many lines as it needs (never cut off); when the title column is taller than the poster, the scores start below the column.
- Search opens full screen on the page background, with the search box where the app title sits. It starts empty each time it's opened (viewing a result and coming back keeps the search). Settings and the detail screen are sheets that slide up over the home screen. A title's detail screen always opens at the top, even if it was scrolled last time; tapping its buttons keeps your place.
- **Nothing moves while browsing.** Like leaves the title where it is; liked titles look unchanged and stay in Top Picks. Not for me and Mark watched are the exceptions (see below). The rows update at natural moments: reopening the app, coming back to it, changing a filter, or closing Settings.
- **"Your library" row** sits right below Top Picks (your recommendations come first) and always shows. Under the title (no subtitle), three pills: **Watchlist** (bookmark), **Liked** (thumb), **Watched** (✓), with the same icons as the poster circles; 14 text, 36 high, 12 side padding, 8 apart, 10 below the title.
  - **Nothing saved, liked or watched yet:** the row is just the title and the pills, none selected; each pill opens that tab on the Your library page.
  - **Updates when the title's screen closes:** saving, liking or marking watched updates the row as soon as the title's screen closes (a poster that no longer belongs under the selected pill fades out and the row closes up first, with a banner; see the feedback rule). Changes made without a title's screen open update it at once. When the row comes out of its empty state, it selects the pill the first title went to (like something first and it opens on Liked). After that the selected pill stays put. The page shifts by any change in the row's height so the row of the title you opened stays exactly where it was.
  - **Otherwise:** the pills switch the row in place, like tabs. The selected pill is white (like the "All" filter); Watchlist is selected each time the app opens. The row shows up to 20 titles (like every home row), most recent first (Watchlist includes watched titles, with the ✓; Watched posters have no ✓), then a "See all ›" tile (poster-sized, card color with a thin outline, 15 semi-bold) that opens Your library at that tab. The home service and TV & movies filters apply.
  - **An empty pill** shows four dashed placeholder tiles, as tall as a poster plus its name (so there is no gap below them); the first says what to tap at 13 with the button name in bold white ("Tap **Like** on any title to see it here").
  - **Titles that leave your services** (or the data) stay in the row and on the Your library page (with "All" selected; not under a single service): the poster is dimmed (the only time a poster is dimmed), with "No longer available" in the middle (12 semi-bold, white, in a dark rounded box 8 in from each side). The service badge stays, showing the service it was on while it was on yours (older saves didn't record it, so they show none). Without a poster, the name sits at the top of a plain tile, above the note. The title's screen says "No longer available" under the year line, and all its buttons still work; one gone from the data has no scores or "Why this pick". Likes still count toward your taste. A title saved from search that was never on your services isn't marked and stays out of the home row. Saves, likes and watched marks keep the poster, year and services for this.
  - The row is the same height in every state (cards reserve room for a two-line name), so switching pills never moves the rows below.
- **Feedback for actions on a title's screen:** if you can see the result on the poster, no banner; if the title disappears, or nothing on screen shows the change, a banner with Undo appears when the screen closes. A title "disappears" when it leaves a home row, a "See all" page, or the pill or tab it was under in Your library (row or page). The banners, by what you did:

  | You… | It leaves… | Banner |
  |---|---|---|
  | Mark watched | home rows; Watchlist pill/tab (page) | Marked as watched |
  | Not for me | home rows; Liked pill/tab. Always shown, even if nothing leaves (never marked on posters) | Marked not for me |
  | Remove from list | Watchlist pill/tab | Removed from your list |
  | Unlike | Liked pill/tab | Like removed |
  | Un-mark watched | Watched pill/tab | Marked as not watched |

  Add to list and Like never get one (the bookmark or thumb shows), and neither does Mark watched when the title stays in the Your library row's Watchlist pill (with the ✓). If several things changed, the banner names the first in the table's order (Not for me first). Undo puts back everything as it was when the title's screen opened. New actions follow the same rule.
- **Not for me and Mark watched remove the title when its screen closes.** While the title's screen is open, they only change the button (so they can be undone in place). On closing, a title newly marked Not for me or watched fades out of every row it leaves (0.15 seconds) and the row closes the gap (0.2 seconds); in a "See all" grid it fades the same way, then the posters after it glide into their new places (0.2 seconds) instead of jumping. Your library and the Netflix Top 10 keep it, and if it's only in those, nothing moves (Not for me still shows its banner; see the feedback rule above). One thing moves at a time: the poster fades (0.15 seconds), then the row closes the gap (0.2 seconds), then, as soon as the row has settled, the banner slides up (so it starts about 0.35 seconds after the screen closes; slower steps with a short beat, about 0.55 seconds, felt slow). Each step waits for the previous one to actually finish, not for a fixed time (fixed timers drifted on the phone and made them overlap). In a "See all" grid, the banner follows the fade. Its 5 seconds count from when it appears. The banner slides up from the bottom: "Marked not for me" (lowercase, reading as a sentence; which wins if both were tapped) or "Marked as watched", on one line. It says only what was tapped, in the buttons' words; the poster leaving the row shows the rest. Tried: "Removed from recommendations" (too abstract), "Disliked and removed" (not the app's words) and "Marked “Not for me” and hidden" (not accurate: the title stays in Your library, the Netflix Top 10 and search). with an "Undo" button on the right, in the same style as the "Update available" banner (card color, thin outline, orange button with dark text). It fades in while rising a little (16 pixels, 0.25 seconds), like the "Update available" banner, enough movement to catch the eye without being a slide (a quicker 0.2-second fade with an 8-pixel rise was easy to miss). After 5 seconds (or Undo) it fades out quickly where it is (0.15 seconds), without sliding; a slide back down was tried. Tried: slides up from below the bottom edge of the screen at 0.45, 0.38, 0.3 and 0.2 seconds (the slide itself didn't feel right), and a slower 0.25-second fade. Undo puts the title back where it was, exactly as it was when its screen opened (its rating, none or Like, and not watched). A second removal replaces the banner, and Undo applies to the latest. If the "Update available" banner is showing, this one sits just above it. Both banners show over whatever is open: the home screen, a "See all" page, Your library, search or Profile.
- The exceptions to "nothing moves" are the ones in the feedback rule: titles leaving a row, page or Your library pill when their screen closes. Every other change waits for a natural moment.
- **One-time rating tip:** the first time someone who hasn't rated anything opens a title, a yellow bubble ("Tap Like or Not for me on any title. Each tap helps tune your Top Picks.") fades in after half a second (until then it can't be seen or tapped, so the buttons show first). It floats over the content just above the Like / Not for me row, with a pointer at the middle of Like, and scrolls with the page.
