How Top picks work

    Profile

    Pick your services, then tap 👍 on a few genres you love.

    Streaming services

    Pick the ones you subscribe to. Everything in the app is limited to these.

    Genres

    Tap 👍 or 👎 for genres you like or don't. They count toward "Your taste" in Top picks, along with your thumbs on titles. Tap again to clear.
