What to Watch

Settings

Your streaming services

Pick the ones you subscribe to. Everything in the app is limited to these.

Genres you like

Optional. Tap 👍 or 👎 for genres you like or don't. They count toward "Your taste" in Top picks, along with your thumbs on titles. Tap again to clear.

Your ratings

Thumbs up and down shape your Top picks. Rated titles are left out of Top picks. Tap ✕ to remove a rating.

    How Top picks are ranked

    Each title gets a score from 0 to 100 for each signal, and these weights combine them.

      Back up your ratings

      Saves your services and ratings to a small file. Keep it in Files, iCloud Drive or an email to yourself, and restore it on a new phone or after clearing your browser.

      Where the data comes from

      • Trending now: Trakt (people watching right now) averaged with TMDB's daily trending list, so titles hot on both rank highest
      • Top rated by audiences: IMDb ratings (adjusted for vote count), tilted 35% toward recent releases, titles with 20K+ IMDb votes
      • Critics' favorites: Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic via OMDb, tilted 25% toward recent releases, titles with 20K+ IMDb votes
      • New on your services: TMDB, with streaming availability from JustWatch
      • Netflix Top 10: Netflix's official weekly list
      • Top picks for you: all of the above plus your taste (thumbs and genre likes)

      This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Streaming availability data provided by JustWatch.
