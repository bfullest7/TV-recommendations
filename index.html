    Genres you like

    Optional. Tap 👍 or 👎 for genres you like or don't. They count toward "Your taste" in Top picks, along with your thumbs on titles. Tap again to clear.

    How it's calculated

    Tap any item to see how it works.

    This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Streaming availability data provided by JustWatch. Critic scores via OMDb and MDBList.

    Back up your data

    Saves your services, ratings, genre likes, watchlist and watched titles to a small file. Keep it in Files, iCloud Drive or an email to yourself, and restore it on a new phone or after clearing your browser.
